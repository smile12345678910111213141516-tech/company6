How It Works

A streamlined process designed for clarity, speed, and certainty.

01.

Submit Your Project Details

Begin by providing high-level information about your solar project via our confidential contact form. Key details include project size (MW), location, and operational status. This initial submission is secure, private, and carries no obligation.

Submit Your Project →
02.

Confidential Evaluation

Our expert acquisitions team will review your submission. We may request additional information like Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs), lease agreements, and production data under a strict Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). We use this to perform a swift but thorough analysis of your asset's financial performance and operational health.

03.

Receive a Formal Offer

Based on our analysis, we will present a formal, written cash offer. Crucially, our offers are not subject to financing contingencies. We have dedicated capital ready for deployment, which eliminates a major hurdle and source of uncertainty common in the market.

04.

Due Diligence & Close

Upon acceptance of our offer, we enter a brief due diligence period to verify all project details. Our experienced team manages this process efficiently to ensure we meet our target closing timeline of 30-60 days. You receive your funds, and we take over as the new, responsible owner of the asset.
