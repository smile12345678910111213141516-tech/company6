Our Mission

Bridging the liquidity gap in clean energy infrastructure.

Who We Are

Repowering Partners was founded to solve a critical issue in the solar market: while development capital is abundant, liquidity for existing small-to-mid-utility scale projects is scarce.

Our team consists of energy finance veterans from major utilities and private equity firms. We understand the technical reality of solar assets and the financial needs of owners.
